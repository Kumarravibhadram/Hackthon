# Common Retail Banking Roles

Retail banking serves individual customers and small businesses through branches, digital channels, contact centres, and operational teams. Common roles include:

- Branch Manager: Leads branch operations, customer service, sales performance, compliance, and staff development.
- Personal Banker or Customer Service Representative: Opens accounts, handles everyday banking requests, and supports customers with products and services.
- Relationship Manager: Builds ongoing customer relationships, understands financial needs, and recommends suitable banking products.
- Teller or Cashier: Processes deposits, withdrawals, payments, cash handling, and basic account requests while following verification controls.
- Loan or Credit Officer: Reviews loan applications, assesses affordability and credit risk, and supports lending decisions.
- Mortgage Advisor: Helps customers understand home-loan options, eligibility, documentation, and application steps.
- Financial Advisor: Provides guidance on savings, investments, insurance, and long-term financial planning within approved advice boundaries.
- Customer Service or Contact-Centre Agent: Resolves account questions, card issues, payments, complaints, and digital-banking support requests.
- Operations or Back-Office Specialist: Processes account maintenance, payments, reconciliations, documentation, and service requests.
- Compliance and Risk Officer: Monitors regulatory requirements, fraud controls, anti-money-laundering checks, customer verification, and operational risk.

Responsibilities vary by bank, but all retail-banking roles must protect customer information, follow approved procedures, complete required identity checks, and escalate suspected fraud or compliance concerns.
