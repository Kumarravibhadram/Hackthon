# Customer Verification Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-15

Before providing account-specific assistance, employees must complete two approved identity checks. Approved checks are a one-time passcode sent to the customer's registered contact method and verification of one additional account detail. Employees must not disclose account information when either check fails. Escalate suspected fraud or repeated verification failure to the Financial Crime Operations team.

Source owner: Retail Operations
Classification: Internal approved guidance
