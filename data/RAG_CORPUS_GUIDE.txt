BANKING EMPLOYEE PRODUCTIVITY – RAG POC CORPUS

Documents are structured as enterprise banking artifacts for testing ingestion,
retrieval, cross-document reasoning, functional analysis and technical analysis.

Recommended metadata:
document_id, document_type, domain, project, department, owner, version,
status, effective_date, confidentiality, source_system.

Example RAG questions:
- What is the end-to-end payment flow?
- What are the payment platform dependencies?
- What decisions were made in the latest project review?
- What risks and actions are currently open?
- What are the functional requirements of the AI copilot?
- What are the KYC manual-review triggers?
- Which APIs are exposed by the Core Banking Adapter?
- Create a functional view of account opening.
- Create a technical view of payment processing.
- Create an executive summary using requirements, architecture and project status.
